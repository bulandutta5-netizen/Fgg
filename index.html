Our Story

Located on the 5th Floor right opposite Quest Mall in Ballygunge, La Letizia is Kolkata's premier coffee lounge and late-night retreat.

Whether you're stopping by at noon for a freshly pulled espresso and artisan café platters, or unwinding with friends at 3:00 AM over signature mocktails and desserts, our vibrant 5th-floor ambiance welcomes you every single day from 12:00 PM to 5:00 AM.
